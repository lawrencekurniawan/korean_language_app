## Public-domain and openly licensed sources

The app may bundle public-domain Korean texts (for example, Korean Wikisource
works whose authors died before 1955, traditional folk tales, and public-domain
government language materials) and openly licensed texts (for example, Korean
Wikipedia under CC BY-SA 4.0). Each bundled article records its source, URL,
licence, and attribution in the app's Library screen. Those texts retain their
original licences.

## Material that is never committed

The following are personal study materials and are excluded from version control
and from public builds:

- Textbook PDFs and page images (Vitamin Korean, Korean Grammar in Use)
- Textbook audio (Vitamin Korean MP3s)
- Subtitle files (SRT/VTT/ASS)
- TOPIK past papers
- Any OCR output derived from the above

They remain on the developer's device and, when bundled into a personal IPA, are
used for personal study only.
